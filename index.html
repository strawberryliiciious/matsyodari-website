Transmission Line Installation

Transmission and distribution lines carry electricity from where it is made to where it is used. We install them with careful planning, trained crews and proper testing.

  • Route survey and layout support
  • Foundation work
  • Tower or pole erection
  • Conductor and earthwire stringing
  • Insulators and line hardware fitting
  • Earthing, testing and charging support

How a line job runs

  1. Survey the routeCheck the path, ground and access.
  2. Build foundations, erect towersA strong base first, then the structure.
  3. String the conductorsPull, tension and fit lines and insulators.
  4. Test and chargeCheck earthing and line, then support energising.

Substation Equipment Installation

A substation controls and steps power up or down. We install and connect its equipment so it works safely from the first day.

  • Power and distribution transformers
  • Circuit breakers and isolators
  • Current and potential transformers
  • Lightning arresters
  • Bus bars and support structures
  • Control and protection panels

Checks before handover

  1. SafeIsolation and earthing checked before any work.
  2. CorrectEquipment fitted as per approved drawings.
  3. TestedReadings noted and tests completed.
  4. DocumentedTest records given to the client.
